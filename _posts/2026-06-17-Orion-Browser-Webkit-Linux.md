## Features Orion Browser
![Orion Beta]({{ site.baseurl }}/images/orion/home2.webp)

Features available on Orion Browser (will have on linux) is:
1. Focus Mode; Hide entire interface to remove distraction (may like fullcreen-mode?)
2. Ad & Tracker Blocking; Block ads and Trackers Natively (Just like that implement on Brave, Waterfox)
3. Tab - Group Switcher; Get visual preview of every open tab
4. Unified sidebar; Your Workspace, organized
