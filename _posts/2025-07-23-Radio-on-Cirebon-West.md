not except Indonesia is now testing the DAB+ at some City available (include Jakara ch 12D RRI Jakarta, Jogjakarta, etc), and DRM (but i dont know what city still test with this method).

but still is can be alternative a public entertainment for poor people, in car, hobbyist, and old people.

## In Cirebon (West)
In Cirebon instead we have Public Boardcast is RRI Cirebon, but since budget savings for goverment we now just can listen RRI on PROGRAME 1 at 94,8FM and 864AM but is some time the AM is offair,
then we have a RRI PROGRAMME 2 at 97,5FM. Programme 3? nope, we dont have this on Cirebon, before 2024 in Cirebon can listen Programme 3 at 99,6FM or 999AM (AM have with tiny signal, before 2023 AM have receiving with strong signal).
other Radio Station we have on Cirebon is: Dairi FM, Pilar Radio, Cirebon Radio, DB Radio, Radio Indah Siar, Kita FM, etc (see Video Above - Record at 2025-07-23)
